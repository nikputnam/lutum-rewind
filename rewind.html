LUTUM Rewind

Resume a paused clay print from where the bead actually stopped.

Loaded from the web, not from the printer

You can use this copy offline: load your G-code, type the nozzle position, click Find and save the resume file.

To control the printer from this copy, enter its address below, and keep this tab open when you switch to the printer's WiFi. The printer must allow pages from other sites to talk to it (CORS): in config.g change M586 P0 S1 to M586 P0 S1 C"*" and restart. Your browser may also ask for permission to reach devices on your local network. Some browsers also block https pages from reaching plain-http devices at all.

If it won't connect, install the page on the printer. That always works:

  1. Download rewind.html.
  2. Copy it to the www folder of the printer's SD card, or run python3 lutum_rewind.py --host <printer-ip> --install-page.
  3. On the printer's WiFi, open http://<printer-ip>/rewind.html.
  1. Pause the print (Duet / RepRapFirmware). Fix the clay feed and prime the nozzle by hand.
  2. Jog the nozzle onto the end of the last good bead, just touching its top.
  3. Connect below, load the job's G-code, click Find, check the match.
  4. Upload the resume file, then Cancel & resume. Don't home or move the axes in between.
